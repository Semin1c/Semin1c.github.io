E-commerce · Side project

Can you trust the data before you trust the answer?

A growth and inventory diagnostic for a US sportswear brand. Most of the work wasn't the analysis — it was finding the nine ways the raw export lied, each of which had already produced a confident wrong conclusion.

What I took from itEvery dataset has traps like these. The difference is whether you find them or your client does, six weeks later.
Churn · Streaming

Who actually cancels a streaming subscription?

I expected price and plan tier to matter most. They barely register — how much someone watches separates leavers from stayers so cleanly that taking viewing behaviour out collapses three different models to near guesswork.

What I took from itFeature importance can be a warning, not a win. When one input carries everything, the interesting question is why the others don't.
People analytics · ROI

Is it worth paying to keep people?

Attrition concentrates far harder than I assumed — two employee groups account for about a third of departures. The part I got wrong first was the money: my initial return was built on an industry benchmark and it was off by an order of magnitude.

What I took from itCosting something properly is slower and less flattering than benchmarking it, and it's the only version you could defend to a CFO.

Hockey · Current

What should a junior hockey programme actually be recording?

Three affiliated programmes with no shared standard for what gets tracked during a game. I'm building that framework with the GMs and coaches rather than handing them a schema.

What I took from itA collection standard nobody follows is worth nothing. Designing it with the people holding the clipboard is the whole job.
Premier League · 8 seasons

Does having the ball more actually create more chances?

I tested 23 possession metrics across eight Premier League seasons. On clubs the model hadn't seen, touches in the opposition box alone did better than the whole set together.

What I took from itMost of what we measure about possession is describing the same thing several times over.
NBA · 10 seasons

How much of a result is decided by the calendar?

Across ten NBA seasons, separating rest, travel and opponent sequencing from how good a team actually is. A meaningful rest edge is worth a lot more than I'd have guessed.

What I took from itSome of what looks like form is just scheduling, and it's measurable before tip-off.
