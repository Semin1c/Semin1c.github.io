Major League Soccer · Europe's big five · 2010–2024

MLS Has More Parity Than Europe's Big Five

Fourteen seasons, six leagues, and two definitions of “competitive” that point in opposite directions.

On competitive balance, MLS beats every one of Europe's big five on two independent measures — and the finding survives splitting the conferences and resampling for league size. A classifier reported at 88–90% accuracy turned out to be riding an 85.6% majority class; corrected, it reaches AUC 0.954.

La Liga · 2023–24 · 380 matches

Where Home Advantage in La Liga Comes From

An effect everyone agrees exists, and the surprisingly short list of things that explain it.

Home sides create 1.484 expected goals to 1.121 while converting at an identical rate, so the edge is territorial rather than finishing. Attendance predicts home points until you control for who fills big stadiums, then it is nothing. The referee spread and the club finishing table both sit inside what chance alone produces.

Premier League · 2017–18 to 2024–25 · 160 squads, 4,343 players

What Follows a Player to a New Club

Possession doesn't create chances. And the number clubs scout on mostly doesn't survive a transfer.

One variable — touches in the opposition box — matches all 23 possession metrics combined, and beats them on clubs the model has never seen. At player level, tracking 168 intra-league transfers, what a player does carries across a move; what it produced mostly doesn't.
